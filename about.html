I am an undergraduate researcher at the University of Tehran pursuing a trajectory spanning analytical economics and foundational philosophy. My primary investigations focus within moral and political philosophy, applied ethics, and the philosophy of action—focusing closely on practical and theoretical reasoning, deliberation, and the structural dynamics of reasons and oughts.
Presently, I am carrying out independent research supervised by Professor Amir Saemi at the Institute for Research in Fundamental Sciences (IPM), examining whether promising places the promisor in a better epistemic position to know that she will do what she promised.
