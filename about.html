Biography & Research Interests

I am a student at the University of Tehran finishing a Bachelor's degree in Economics alongside a minor track in Philosophy[cite: 1]. My academic research centers primarily within moral and political philosophy, applied ethics, and the philosophy of action[cite: 1]. More specifically, my interests focus on practical and theoretical reasoning, moral reasons, the architecture of reasons and oughts, deliberation, and rationality[cite: 1].

Currently, I am conducting independent research in collaboration with Professor Amir Saemi at the Institute for Research in Fundamental Sciences (IPM)[cite: 1]. Our work investigates whether the act of promising inherently updates a promisor's epistemic status, developing a critique against contemporary accounts of the knowledge norm of promising[cite: 1].
