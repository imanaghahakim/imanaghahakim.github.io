Promising as an Epistemic Practice

Iman Aghahakim, University of Tehran

I. Introduction

Promises play a crucial role in our moral and social practices[cite: 1]. By promising, we undertake obligations, generate expectations, and make ourselves accountable to others[cite: 1]. Moorean promises, however—a specific kind of promise—appear puzzling[cite: 1]. Such promises seem to involve a weak commitment, which makes them sound absurd, given that we ordinarily expect promises to involve strong commitment[cite: 1].
